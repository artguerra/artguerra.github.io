About

I’m a Computer Science & Engineering student focused on computer graphics (some topics below!). I’m also interested in computer vision and sometimes use it alongside my graphics work.

Day to day, I use mainly TS/WGSL and C++/OpenGL/GLSL to make projects and prototypes. I prefer simple and usable tools which still offer me a lot of control. I really like supporting open-source projects, so I prioritize these as well.

Some interests: image synthesis (rendering), geometry processing & modeling, real-time rendering, simulation, procedural generation.

Just finished an internship at Adobe Research (Paris) and my Master's degree; currently looking for PhD opportunities!
